Jas — the ground

Five living grounds

The ground the brief describes: cellular and biomorphic patterns that grow, drift and respond. Hundreds of cells a frame, each with its own ink, its own drift and its own slow swelling — then raked and curled like a marbling tray, which is where the wash comes from.

Five tiles, five of the things board 1 names: cell structures, mycelial networks, moiré, fractal growth, reaction-diffusion. Texture is applied to the pigment rather than laid over it, so nothing sits still while the fluid moves underneath. Drag any tile to work it.
